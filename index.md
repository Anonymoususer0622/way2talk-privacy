
# Privacy Policy for way2talk

**Last updated:** August 13, 2026

**way2talk** ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information.

---

### 1. Information We Collect and Process
- **Audio & Speech Data:** way2talk accesses your device's microphone solely to capture spoken audio when you actively trigger a translation. Audio is processed into text via Speech-to-Text services and sent to processing APIs (such as OpenRouter) to generate accurate translations. Audio recordings are **never stored** on external servers or used to train public models.
- **Local Translation History:** Your translated conversations are stored **locally on your device** using an encrypted local SQLite database. We do not transmit or store your translation history on external servers.
- **Device Information:** Non-personal technical data (such as OS version and language preferences) may be processed locally to ensure the app operates smoothly.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services
To provide real-time translation and voice synthesis, way2talk utilizes trusted third-party providers:
- **OpenRouter API:** Processes text prompts strictly for returning language translations.
- **Google Speech-to-Text / Text-to-Speech:** Used locally on your device for speech recognition and audio playback.

Each third-party service processes data in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

---

### 3. Data Storage & Security
All conversation history remains stored locally on your device. You can clear your entire translation history at any time directly through the app settings or by clearing the app data on your device.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy
way2talk does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
